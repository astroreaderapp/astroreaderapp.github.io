Astro Reader Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

In short:

  • Your books, highlights, notes, progress and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • You don't need an account to read. Accounts are only used for Astro Reader Pro.
  • Currently, the app shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell your data.
  • Some features use the internet; below we explain which ones and what they send.

1. Who we are

Astro Reader is an Android app for reading PDF and EPUB books. It is developed by Siriux Labs. If you have questions about this policy, write to us at contacto.astroreader@gmail.com.

2. What stays only on your phone

The following is stored inside the app, on your phone, and as of the date of this policy it is not sent to any server or synced to the cloud:

  • The books you import (the app keeps a copy in its own storage) and their covers.
  • Your highlights, personal notes and bookmarks.
  • Your reading progress, statistics, streak, achievements and collections.
  • Your reading journal: ratings, quotes, reviews and any cover photos you add.
  • Your settings (theme, font, language, reminder and so on).

The app does not make cloud backups: if you switch phones, this data does not move to the new one.

3. Your account (optional, Pro only)

You don't need an account to read. If you choose to create one for Astro Reader Pro (so you can get Pro back if you switch phones or reinstall the app), we store:

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, stored encrypted: no one, including us, can read it.
  • Your username.
  • Your Pro purchases: a 6-digit purchase code, the date, the plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime), the price and, if applicable, the end date.

This data is stored with Supabase, a cloud database service with servers in the United States, which processes it on our behalf. Your email is used only for your account: signing in and sending you a code if you ask to change your password. Those emails are sent from contacto.astroreader@gmail.com through Gmail.

4. Payments

Pro payments are processed by Google Play. Astro Reader never sees or stores your card or payment details; it only receives confirmation of the purchase to activate Pro on your account.

5. News notifications

If you agree to receive notifications, the app registers your phone's notification identifier, together with the app language, whether you have Pro and, if you are signed in, your account. It is stored with Supabase and used only to send you news about the app. Notifications are delivered through the Expo notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google).

You can turn them off at any time in Settings › News notifications, or in your phone's notification settings. The daily reading reminder is different: your own phone creates it and it does not use the internet.

6. Features that use the internet

These features connect to the internet only when you use them, and send only what is needed:

  • Dictionary: sends the word you look up to Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation) to get its definition.
  • Free books: the book list is loaded from our database and books are downloaded from Project Gutenberg.
  • Journal song: what you type in the search box is sent to Apple's search service (iTunes) to show songs. If you tap one, it opens in Spotify or YouTube.
  • Paper books: what you type in the search box is sent to Open Library (Internet Archive) to find the book and its cover.
  • Pro prices and in-app news: the app loads them from our database without sending any of your data.
  • Reviews: when you finish a book, the app may show the Google Play review window. That window is handled by Google.
  • Listen to the book and read aloud: use the voices installed on your phone. Some voices from your phone's speech service (for example, Google's) may use the internet, depending on how you have set them up.

7. Camera and photos

The app asks for your camera or photos only if you want to add a cover to a paper book in your journal. The photo is stored on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

8. What we don't do

As of the date of this policy:

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

9. How your data is deleted

  • When you delete a book from the app, its file, cover, highlights, notes and bookmarks are deleted. If the book has an entry in your journal, the entry keeps its title, author and cover until you remove it.
  • When you uninstall the app, everything stored on your phone is deleted.
  • To delete your account: in the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete my account. You can also email contacto.astroreader@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject “Delete my account”; we will delete it within 30 days and let you know by email.

Deleting your account erases your email, password and username. For accounting purposes, the record of each sale (code, date, plan and price) is kept without any data that identifies you. If you have a subscription, also cancel it in Google Play (Play Store › your photo › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.

10. How long we keep data

Your account data is kept for as long as you have an account. The notification identifier is kept while you have the app installed with notifications turned on.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data by writing to contacto.astroreader@gmail.com.

12. Children

Astro Reader is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

13. Changes to this policy

Before adding new features that change how the app handles your data, we will update this policy on this same page and change the date above. If the change is important, we will also let you know in the app.
